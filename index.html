See all your animated GIFs at once

Trying to view many animated GIFs on Windows and macOS sucks donkey balls. This web app lets you open a folder and view all the GIFs as big grid that you can scroll, zoom in, pause at any frame, and handle loading even browsing 1000s of GIFs without breaking a sweat!

Nothing is uploaded — files are read locally in your browser.
Click to zoomScroll to scale 1–20×, or fill the screen
Pause anythingFreeze a GIF right where it is, then step frame by frame
Backdrop colorsCheckerboard or solid, to check transparent edges

Controls

Good to know

  • Only .gif files are loaded — everything else in the folder is ignored.
  • Tick Subfolders before opening to include nested folders.
  • Handles thousands of GIFs; only what's on screen animates.
  • Favorites and settings are remembered. Paused GIFs are not.
  • Works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.
